Pills & Tablet Reminder · Questions and answers

My reminders don't arrive

Open the iOS Settings app › Notifications › Pill Reminder and make sure Allow Notifications is on. Check that a Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't silencing them. Then open the app once, so it can set up your reminders again. If reminders are off, the app shows a banner on the Today screen with a button to fix it.

How do I mark a dose from the notification?

Press and hold the notification (or swipe left and tap View) to see Taken, Snooze and Skip.

How do I change the language?

In the app, go to Settings › Language and pick one of 40 languages, or "Same as iPhone". The app switches straight away.

How do I make the text bigger?

Go to Settings › Big text. The app also follows the text size set in the iOS Settings app › Display & Brightness › Text Size.

How do I add a family member?

Go to Settings › Add Family Member (Pro). Each person has their own medicines, and you can filter the Today screen by person.

How does the caregiver text work?

Add a contact with a phone number in Health › Contacts and set them as caregiver. When a dose is missed, the reminder has a "Tell my caregiver" button that opens Messages with a ready-made text.

I'm getting a new iPhone

Your data moves with a normal iPhone backup or Quick Start. You can also go to Settings › Export Backup, save the file to Files or iCloud Drive, and use Restore from Backup on the new iPhone.

Restore my purchase

Open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases. Pro is $1.99 a year or $2.99 once, and works on the iPhones signed in with the same Apple ID.

Cancel my subscription

Open the iOS Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › Pills & Tablet Reminder › Cancel Subscription. For a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
