Privacy Policy

Pills & Tablet Reminder · Effective 27 November 2026

The short version

Pills & Tablet Reminder (shown as "Pill Reminder" on your iPhone) does not collect, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. We run no servers and can't see your medicines, doses or health readings. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores, and where

The people you add, their medicines and schedules, dose history, stock counts, health readings, appointments, journal entries and contacts are saved only on your iPhone, inside the app's private storage. The home screen widget reads the same data on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded to us or to any other company.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your iPhone. They are not sent through any server. The notification can show the medicine name; you can hide previews in the iOS Settings app › Notifications.

Photos

You can add a photo of a medicine. The app only reads the single photo you choose, and it doesn't need access to your photo library. The photo is stored with the medicine on your iPhone.

Face ID

If you turn on the Face ID lock, Face ID or your passcode is checked by iOS. The app never receives your face data.

Caregiver text messages

If a dose is missed, you can choose to text a caregiver. The app opens Messages with the text filled in; you decide whether to send it. The message goes through your own phone service or iMessage, not through us.

Doctor report and backups

A doctor report (PDF) or a backup file is made only when you ask, and it goes only where you choose to send or save it, such as Files, Mail, AirDrop or iCloud Drive. It doesn't pass through us. Your data can also be part of your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup, depending on your device settings. We have no access to these backups.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything in the app under Settings › Delete All Data, or by deleting the app.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. You can manage or cancel a subscription in the iOS Settings app under your name › Subscriptions.

Email

If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply. We do not add you to any mailing list.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Not medical advice

The app is a reminder tool. It doesn't give medical advice. Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
